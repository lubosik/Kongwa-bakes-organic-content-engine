## How concepts are scored

Each criterion is 1 to 5. Total out of 30.

| Criterion | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Evidence | 3 or more researched examples, well above their own median, close to our situation |
| Faceless | hands only, or no people at all |
| Product | our real bake on screen most of the time |
| Red carpet | next step to order is obvious and exists today |
| Cost | no credits, filmable in under 15 minutes, or already have the footage |
| Risk | far from every edge of the compliance gate (brief 5.1) |

Mechanic codes (M1 to M10) refer to `research/summary.md`.

## Standard caption sign-off (every post)

The research says the caption is where orders come from, and the order line costs no reach (1.0x of median with or without it). So every post ends with the same block:

> Orders close Thursday 5pm.
> Collection or local delivery in Medway, Saturday 8am to midday.
> ORDER at the link in bio.

Cake posts swap in: "Celebration cakes need 10 days. Tell us about yours at the link in bio."
Catering posts swap in: "Events need 14 days. Tell us what you need at the link in bio."

- **Shot list:**
