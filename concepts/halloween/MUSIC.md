# Halloween music: trending sounds and Suno prompts

For `HW-04_uncle-ditch-too-far_v3`: the bait (0 to 16.15s) carries real foley only (doorbell, door, "Hello? Who is there?", slippers, tiptoes, hallway steps, BOO). The music starts **on the switch at 16.15s** and runs to the end (about 8.4s). Pick a track whose **first beat is a big hit or drop**, and line that beat up with the cut.

## 1. Trending sounds right now (check in-app before posting)

SAYS (New Engen, October 2026, one source, a snapshot not live data): [Halloween TikTok and Reels trends](https://newengen.com/insights/halloween-tiktok-reels-trends/), [October 2026 TikTok trends](https://newengen.com/insights/october-tiktok-trends/)

- **"Ramalama (Bang Bang)" by Róisín Murphy** is the top Halloween sound, used for costume reveals (low-angle walk, then pull back). Its drop is a natural fit for our switch to the bakes.
- Evergreen Halloween sounds also resurge every October: "Spooky, Scary Skeletons" by Andrew Gold (about 3 million videos, [Tokchart](https://tokchart.com/sounds/andrew-gold-spooky-scary-skeletons_6602666381370460933)) and the Addams Family theme.

**Licensing rule:**

- TikTok **business** accounts can only use the **Commercial Music Library** (CML). Mainstream songs like the ones above are often not cleared for business accounts.
- If @kongwa.bakes is a business account, open the sound picker and search "Halloween" in commercial sounds, or use our own Suno track below.
- On Instagram, business accounts get a reduced music catalogue. If a song is greyed out, it is not licensed for us.
- Never rip audio from another creator's video. Brief rule 7.

**How to check what is trending today (2 minutes):**

1. TikTok: Creative Center, then Trends, then Songs, filtered to United Kingdom, last 7 days, "Approved for business use".
2. Instagram: in Reels audio, look for the small upward arrow next to a sound (trending).
3. Pick one with a hard drop in its first second, and set the start point in the app so the drop lands on the switch frame.

## 2. Suno prompts (our own track, zero licensing risk)

Suno's free plan is non-commercial. Generate on **Pro or Premier** so Kongwa Bakes owns the commercial rights.

House method (from `docs/house-standards.md`): Instrumental on, a Style block plus a bar-timed Structure block, target length stated. We fit the edit to the real track, not the other way round.

### A. Afro-Halloween bounce (recommended: matches the uncle, nobody else will have it)

**Style**

> Instrumental, Halloween amapiano and afrobeats crossover, 112 BPM, D minor, deep log drum bass, shakers and congas, spooky church organ stabs, pizzicato strings, a theremin melody, playful and cheeky not scary, warm and danceable, crisp modern mix, punchy low end for phone speakers

**Structure**

> [Intro: 1 bar silence then a huge hit with log drum and organ stab on beat 1]
> [Groove: 4 bars, log drum and shaker pattern, organ riff, theremin hook]
> [Build: 2 bars, toms and rising organ]
> [Drop: 4 bars, full groove, hook doubled on pizzicato]
> [Button ending: single organ stab and log drum hit, then silence]
>
> Length: 0:30

### B. Spooky funk drop (closest to the trending "costume reveal" energy)

**Style**

> Instrumental, spooky funk and disco, 120 BPM, E minor, slap bass, harpsichord riff, Hammond organ, handclaps, four-on-the-floor kick, glittery synth stabs, campy Halloween fun, bright and bouncy, TikTok-ready, big first-beat impact

**Structure**

> [Intro: instant drop on beat 1, full band, no build-up]
> [Hook: 8 bars, harpsichord and slap bass riff]
> [Break: 2 bars, organ only]
> [Hook return: 4 bars]
> [Button ending: stab and cymbal choke]
>
> Length: 0:30

### C. Halloween trap hit (for the meme versions)

**Style**

> Instrumental, Halloween trap, 140 BPM half-time feel, 808 slides, music-box melody, eerie choir pad, crunchy hi-hat rolls, comedic horror, phone-speaker friendly, loud first beat

**Structure**

>
> Length: 0:30

## 3. How to drop it in

- In CapCut or the TikTok editor, put the track's first big hit exactly on **16.15s** (the first frame of the brownies).
- Keep our foley under the bait. Do not run music under the prank: the silence is what makes it feel like real doorbell footage.
- Music level: full on the switch, then sitting comfortably under the end card. No ducking or pumping (house rule).
