# Halloween bait-and-switch (HW series)

Brief from Bosi, 29 October 2026: start like a brain-rot prank meme, build to the highest point of tension, then cut to our real Halloween bakes before the payoff. POV caption, trending sound.

## Why this can work (evidence)

- **SAYS:** a scripted doorbell-camera "ding dong ditch" sketch reached more than 94 million views in five days (posted 30 July 2025). It was staged, which shows the format does not need real footage. [Lead Stories](https://leadstories.com/hoax-alert/2025/08/fact-check-video-boy-dad-father-ding-dong-ditch-ditching.html)
- **SAYS:** Ring ran a Halloween influencer campaign built on spooky doorbell skits. [Campaign Live](https://campaignlive.com/article/case-study-ring-influencer-campaign-embraces-spooky-side-doorbell-cams/1900685)
- **DATA (our research):** fake jeopardy then reveal measured 44x to 1,021x the account median (Wants and Needs, `research/summary.md` M8). The switch landed at 50 to 64% of runtime.
- **THINK:** the doorbell-cam look does three jobs. It is a format people already stop for, the grain hides AI artefacts, and black and white makes the switch to colourful bakes hit harder.

## Why we do not repost other people's prank clips

Bosi suggested finding prank clips on YouTube (for example African mum or dad Halloween pranks) and switching to our bakes.

We use those for timing reference only, never in the post:

- Copyright: it is someone else's video in our commercial ad; takedowns and strikes follow.
- Real, identifiable people (often children) in an ad without consent.
- TikTok and Instagram down-rank reposted, unoriginal content, which kills the reach we are after.
- Brief rule 7 and the realism protocol (Level 3): never other creators' clips, never real people scraped from social media.

We get the same mechanic with our own footage: AI shots with fully masked synthetic characters now, and family-filmed versions next.

## The story beats (what makes a prank readable)

Bosi's note on v1: the viewer has to be **in on the prank**. We see the kid hide, we see the victim miss it, we know the scare is coming, and we cut right before it lands.

| Beat | HW-03 doorbell sneak-in |
|---|---|
| Set-up | kid in a ghost sheet rings the bell and hides beside the door, in view of the camera |
| Victim misses it | man opens the door, walks down the path looking for who rang |
| Escalation | the ghost tiptoes into the house behind his back; he comes back in and shuts the door |
| Peak | inside cam: he walks down the hallway, the ghost is crouched behind the kitchen door ready to jump |
| Switch | glitch, dead air, boom: "POV: ..." over the real Halloween bakes |
| End card | KB monogram, one line, CTA |

## HW-08: family-filmed version (0 credits, Level 1 realism)

Consenting family only. 15 minutes. Phone in the hallway corner on a shelf or tripod, night, lights off, phone torch or a lamp at the end of the hall.

1. Wide static shot: the hallway, kitchen door half open at the end. Bosi in any Halloween mask crouches behind the kitchen door (visible to camera).
2. Mum walks down the hall towards the kitchen, unaware. Keep walking, slowly.
3. Bosi starts to rise. Stop recording 1 second after he starts moving. Do not film the scare or the reaction (we cut before it anyway, and nobody is filmed upset).
4. Then, in the kitchen with the lights on: 4 shots of the Halloween bakes, 3 seconds each, slow move in.

Mum must agree to be filmed and to it being posted. A face is fine if she is happy with that, or film from behind.

## Rules for every HW post

- AI label on for every AI shot.
- Masked or costumed characters only, no identifiable faces, no real people.
- The scare is play: no weapons, no blood, no injury, no break-in, no door-kicking (the "door knock challenge" has caused real damage, which we will not echo).
- KB label in the first second and the hook from frame 1, so it reads as content, not real footage (ASA CAP 2.1, 2.3, 4.2).
- Real bakes only after the switch (`assets/library/raw-2026-10-28/`).
