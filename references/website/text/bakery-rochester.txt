Collection
Rochester sits at the heart of what we cover, so collection is straightforward. You order by the Thursday and pick up on the Saturday between 8am and midday. We send the exact address with your confirmation email.
Delivery to Rochester
Every Rochester postcode is inside our delivery area, and you are close enough that delivery is free on orders over twenty five pounds. We bring it on the Saturday morning and ring before we set off.

Questions from Rochester
Do you deliver to Rochester?
Yes, and Rochester is the closest part of our round, so delivery is free on orders over twenty five pounds. Put your postcode into the basket and it will tell you the exact charge, if any, before you pay.
Can I collect in Rochester on a weekday?
Not as a shop, no, and it is worth being straight about why. There is no counter to walk into and nothing sitting out waiting for you. Collection and delivery are a named morning, and for almost everybody that morning is the Saturday. If you genuinely need an order earlier in the week, the checkout lists the earlier days we can still manage on the same notice, and you pick one there.
Can you do a larger order for a Rochester festival weekend?
Yes, and we would rather know early. Festival weekends get busy, so use the catering form rather than the normal basket and we will build a menu and a price around your event.
