Collection
Collection runs Saturday from 8am to midday. Rainham is the far end of Medway from where we bake, so if you would rather not make the trip, delivery is the easier option.
Delivery to Rainham
Rainham and Rainham Mark are both covered, on the Saturday morning, on orders over twenty five pounds. Being the far end of Medway from us, Rainham carries a small delivery charge of £2.50 rather than being free, which is the honest price of the extra distance. Questions from Rainham
I am on the edge of Rainham. Do you still deliver?
Put your postcode into the basket and it will tell you before you pay. We check it against the real Medway boundary rather than guessing from the first half of the postcode, because ME8 runs across more than one district.
Is Rainham too far for delivery?
No. It is the furthest part of Medway from us, which is why it carries a £2.50 delivery charge rather than being free, but it is inside the area and we come every Saturday.
Do you deliver to Rainham in Essex or Rainham in London?
No, and it is worth saying plainly because there are three Rainhams. We only cover Rainham in Kent, the one in Medway with an ME8 postcode.
