Questions from Gillingham
Do you deliver to Hempstead and Wigmore?
Yes. They are further out than the rest of the round but still inside Medway, so they are covered. Type your postcode into the basket and it will confirm before you pay.
Do you deliver on Gillingham home match days?
Yes, but we plan the route around it. If you are close to Priestfield and there is a home game, expect us earlier in the window rather than later.
What if my postcode is ME7 but I am nearer Rainham?
It can, slightly. Delivery is free over twenty five pounds across most of Medway, but the far eastern edge past Rainham carries a small charge for the extra distance. The basket tells you which you are before you pay.
