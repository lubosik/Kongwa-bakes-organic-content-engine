Our Story
Kongwa Bakes is run by The Kongwa Family from our kitchen in Medway, Kent. Hilary is head chef, shaping every loaf by hand with real care and no shortcuts.
We are not trying to be a big brand pretending to be small. We are a small family doing this properly: real fermentation, real time, and bakes that taste like someone actually made them, because someone did.
The finishing touch
Wrapped as carefully as it's baked
Every order leaves in a kraft paper wrap carrying the Kongwa Bakes mark, the same care on the outside as what's baked inside.
Proof, not promises
Already trusted for the big days
Catered for a full corporate grazing table, breads, cheeses, charcuterie and pastries, served fresh for a Medway office event. Every order goes out boxed and labelled with what is in it, whether it is one loaf or a table for forty.
