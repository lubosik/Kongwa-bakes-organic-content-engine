A bakery in Kent that bakes for you, not for a shelf
Kongwa Bakes is a family bakery in Medway. Everything is made to order for the person who ordered it. Nothing is baked ahead for a shelf, nothing is sold down over a week, and nothing sits in a warehouse. Order by the Thursday and collect on the Saturday between 8am and midday, or have it delivered free across Medway on orders over £25.00.

What makes it different from supermarket bread
Slow fermentation, and time. A supermarket sourdough is often made in a few hours with added yeast and flavouring to imitate what a long ferment does on its own. Ours takes days, because that is how long the process takes when you do not shortcut it. It is also why we need your order by the Thursday rather than the night before.

Common questions
Where in Kent do you deliver?
Across Medway: Rochester, Chatham, Gillingham, Strood and Rainham, plus the villages inside the Medway boundary. We check your postcode against the real local authority boundary at checkout rather than guessing from the postcode prefix, because ME7 and ME8 both run across more than one district.
Can I order from elsewhere in Kent?
Yes, for collection. Anyone can order and collect from Medway on a Saturday morning, wherever they live. Delivery is the part limited to Medway, because it is the family driving, not a courier.
Why is it mostly Saturdays?
Because it is a family bakery rather than a shop with staff. One handover a week, done properly, means everything is made for you and genuinely fresh on the day you get it, instead of being baked ahead and sold down over a week. If you need something before the Saturday, the checkout will show you the earlier days we can still manage.
Can I collect on a day that is not a Saturday?
Often, yes. Saturday is the bake and it is what most people choose, but the checkout also lists the earlier days in the week that we can still get to, and you pick one there rather than having to ring. It needs the same notice a Saturday does, so if the day you want is not on the list it is because the dough could not be ready in time.
How far ahead do I need to order?
Order by the Thursday for that Saturday. Anything later rolls to the following week. An earlier day in the week needs the same two clear days' notice, and the checkout tells you exactly which day you are ordering for before you pay.
