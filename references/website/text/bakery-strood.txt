Questions from Strood
Do you cross the river to deliver to Strood?
Yes, every Saturday. Strood being on the other bank makes no difference to whether we come or what you pay.
Do you deliver to Frindsbury and Wainscott?
Yes, both sit inside Medway and inside the delivery area. The basket checks your postcode against the real boundary before you pay, so you will know for certain rather than guessing.
Can I collect early if I am catching a train?
The window opens at 8am and we are there from the start of it. Tell us in the notes roughly when you are coming and we will have it ready and bagged.
